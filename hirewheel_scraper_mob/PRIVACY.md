# Privacy Policy — Hirewatch

**Last updated: 1 November 2026**

Hirewatch is an unofficial app that watches your own HireWheel account for new
opportunities and tells you when something changes. It is **not affiliated
with, endorsed by, or operated by Code2College**.

## Everything stays on your phone

Hirewatch has no server. It talks to exactly one place: **HireWheel itself**.
Nothing the app stores is sent to the app's author or anyone else.

| Data | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **HireWheel session cookies** | So the app can load your pages, including in the background | iPhone Keychain |
| **Your HireWheel login email** | Shown in Settings so you know which account is connected | App storage on your phone |
| **Extracted page contents** | Detecting what changed between scans | App storage on your phone |
| **Saved copies of your HireWheel pages** | The history: seeing a page as it was before a change | App storage on your phone, saved only when a page changes |

Your **HireWheel password is never seen or stored by this app**. You type it into
HireWheel's own login page, shown in a web view, and it goes straight to
HireWheel. Only the session cookies that sign-in creates are kept.

The HireWheel account page also displays a **parent/guardian email address**. The
app reads only the address labelled "Current login email", so the guardian's
address is never read or stored. It belongs to someone who never agreed to use
this tool.

## What is not collected

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting, no location, no
contacts. Nothing is sold or shared.

Notifications are created on the phone itself. Apple's push service is not used.

TestFlight is run by Apple. If you choose to share crash reports or feedback
through it, Apple's terms cover that, not this policy.

## Retention and deletion

Scans that found no changes are deleted after 3 days. Scans that recorded changes
are kept, because they are the history the app exists to provide. At most 60
saved copies are kept per page; older ones are deleted.

**Settings → Sign out of Hirewheel** removes the stored session and keeps your
history. **Settings → Sign out and delete all data** removes everything:
session, scans, saved pages and email. Deleting the app removes the scans and
saved pages, but iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted, so use
"Sign out" first if you want the session gone too.

## Students under 18

HireWheel is an educational platform, and much of what this app copies is student
educational data. Code2College's own
[Privacy Policy](https://www.hirewheel.ai/privacy) describes FERPA alignment,
school coordinator oversight for minors, and audit logging of data access.
Hirewatch keeps that copy only on the student's own phone.
